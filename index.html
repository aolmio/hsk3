Reference

Need-to-know facts

The mechanics of Mandarin and the HSK system that every learner trips over.

Tone sandhi (变调)

  • 3rd + 3rd: the first becomes 2nd. 你好 nǐ hǎo → ní hǎo. With three or more in a row the grouping depends on phrasing; this app changes all but the last.
  • 不 bù becomes bú before a 4th tone: 不是 bú shì. Between a repeated verb it is neutral: 好不好 hǎo bu hǎo.
  • 一 yī becomes yí before a 4th tone (一个 yí gè) and yì before 1st–3rd (一天 yì tiān). It stays yī in counting, ordinals and dates (第一, 十一, 一月).
  • Neutral tone is short and light, with no mark: 妈妈 māma, 我们 wǒmen.

HSK 2.0 vs 3.0 vs 3.1

  • HSK 2.0: six levels and about 5,000 words in total.
  • HSK 3.0 (2021 standard): nine levels in three stages, with levels 7–9 sharing one advanced exam; about 11,092 words.
  • HSK 3.1: a later revision of the 3.0 lists. Some entries mark optional parts in parentheses, such as 没（有）.
  • Use the syllabus filter inside each level to see which list a word belongs to.

The three de (的 / 得 / 地)

  • 的 links a modifier to a noun: 我的书 (my book).
  • 得 follows a verb to describe degree: 说得很好 (speaks very well).
  • 地 turns a word into an adverb before a verb: 认真地学习 (study seriously).

Characters & stroke order

  • Most characters pair a meaning radical (氵water, 扌hand) with a sound component.
  • Stroke order: top to bottom, left to right, horizontal before vertical, outside before inside, close the frame last.
  • Writing each character by hand a few times makes recognition far faster. Use the worksheet generator.

How to study with this app

  • Pick a level, star unfamiliar words and look them up in the detail panel.
  • Rate flashcards honestly; the review queue schedules each word for you.
  • Paste real texts into the analyzer and save the words that are just above your level.
  • Hide pinyin or meanings in the word list to test yourself.
